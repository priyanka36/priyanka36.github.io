I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at **Mississippi State University**, advised by [**Dr. Jingdao Chen**](https://www.cse.msstate.edu/people/faculty/jingdao-chen/). I am also a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Data Science Program, where I work with [Dr. Jonathan Barlow](https://datascience.msstate.edu/directory/jhb11).

My research focuses on **multimodal AI**, **computer vision**, and **robotics**: building systems that combine vision, language, and other sensor data so that robots and AI agents can perceive, understand, and act in the real world. My recent publications also cover federated learning, medical image segmentation, and the evaluation of AI agents.

Before my PhD, I received an M.S. in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence from the University of Missouri–St. Louis and a B.E. in Computer Engineering from Tribhuvan University, Nepal. In between, I spent several years in industry R&D, building computer vision and NLP systems at Fusemachines and the National Innovation Centre, Nepal.

I'm always happy to talk about research and collaborations. Feel free to [email me](mailto:poudelnipriyanka@gmail.com).
